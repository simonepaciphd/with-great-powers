### Research questions *(DRAFT — researcher to confirm / edit)*
1. What is the best available estimate of AI-tool adoption among academic researchers in 2024–2026, with the finest available breakdowns by field, career stage, and country / institution type?
2. What is the evidence on the **depth** of adoption — how many researchers use consumer chat interfaces (ChatGPT, Claude chat) vs. research-grade or agentic workflows (IDE-integrated agents, API use, custom pipelines), and how is this gap evolving?
