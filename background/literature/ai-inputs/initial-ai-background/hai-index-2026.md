## Usable data points

### Closed vs. open weights gap — **outline §3.1**
- As of March 2026, top closed model leads top open model by **3.3%**, up from **0.5%** in August 2024.
- Interpretation: the gap widened over ~18 months — supports the paper's framing that closed-weights retain a performance lead while open-weights "lag a bit" (per outline language).
- Cross-reference with Spirling's normative argument for open-weights use despite the gap.

### Frontier competition — **outline §3.1 (3+ providers)**
- March 2026 Arena Elo leaders: Anthropic (1,503), xAI (1,495), Google (1,494), OpenAI (1,481), Alibaba (1,449), DeepSeek (1,424).
- Supports "3+ frontier providers with intense market competition."

### US/China dynamic
- "U.S. and Chinese models have traded places at the top of the performance rankings multiple times since early 2025."
- As of March 2026, "Anthropic's top model leads by just 2.7%" (over the top Chinese model).

### Adoption (general population) — useful framing for §1.1
- "Generative AI reached 53% population adoption within three years" — faster than PC or internet.
- US: 28.3% population adoption (ranks 24th globally).
- Not researcher-specific — for that see `adoption-statistics.md`.

### AI in science
- "AI-related publications in the natural, physical, and life sciences all increased 26% to 28% year over year."
- Useful proxy for §1.1 research-uptake story.

### Transparency concern — relevant to §4.2
- Foundation Model Transparency Index "dropped to 40 points from last year's 58."
- "Most capable models disclose least information."
- This is an opportunity hook: the paper argues for radical transparency at the **research** level, which is all the more important as the **model** level becomes more opaque.

### Investment / power / environmental (context, possibly for §4.1 "things I don't address")
- Global corporate AI investment: $581.7B in 2025 (+130% YoY). US $285.9B; China $12.4B.
- Grok 4 training: 72,816 tons CO2e. Data-center power: 29.6 GW.
