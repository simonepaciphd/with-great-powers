# Political efficacy and turnout — draft

## Introduction (partial)

The question of whether political efficacy drives turnout has been asked in every major wave of survey-based political behavior scholarship since the 1960s. The answer is persistently "yes — partially." Efficacy correlates with turnout; efficacy also correlates with partisan strength; partisan strength correlates with turnout. What remains unresolved is whether efficacy does work of its own, or whether it serves as a proxy for partisan intensity that the usual covariates fail to capture. The 2024 ANES, with its standard battery of internal and external efficacy items, offers a recent cut at the question.
