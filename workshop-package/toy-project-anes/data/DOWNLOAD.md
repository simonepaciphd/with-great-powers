# Downloading the ANES 2024 data

The American National Election Studies (ANES) distributes its data under a user agreement that requires each individual user to register. We cannot ship the raw CSV in this package, but the codebook (in this folder) is yours to read.

## Steps

1. Go to [https://electionstudies.org/data-center/2024-time-series-study/](https://electionstudies.org/data-center/2024-time-series-study/).
2. Create a free account and accept the ANES User Agreement.
3. Download the **2024 Time Series Study — CSV** zip (named `anes_timeseries_2024_csv_YYYYMMDD.zip`).
4. Extract the CSV (`anes_timeseries_2024_csv_YYYYMMDD.csv`) into this folder, next to the codebook.

The rest of the workshop works identically to the other two toy projects once the file is in place.

## Variable of interest for the workshop

**Political efficacy** — a classic latent construct with both *internal* (can I understand and influence politics?) and *external* (does government respond to people like me?) components. The ANES 2024 instrument carries several efficacy items across the pre- and post-election waves; the codebook documents them. Your job during the session is to pick a measurement strategy and defend its validity and reliability.
